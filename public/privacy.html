Section 1

1. Overview & Commitment

At Pencraft Academy, founded by Osuigwe Wisdom Munachimso (Prince Noble), we hold integrity, respect, and responsibility as foundational pillars. We recognize the importance of your personal data and are dedicated to treating your information with the highest standard of ethical stewardship.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, submit an application form, access the Student Portal, interact with our Penny AI assistant, or communicate with us on official channels like WhatsApp, and how we keep that information safe.

Core Pledge: We collect only the information necessary to provide an authentic, high-quality learning experience, verify course completion, and issue legitimate academic credentials.

Section 2

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you interact with our programs:

  • Application & Enrollment Details: First Name, Last Name, WhatsApp / Phone Number, Email Address, Chosen Course (e.g. Digital Marketing), and your reasons for enrolling.
  • Academic & Assessment Records: Assigned Student ID (e.g. PEN-2026-XXXX), course progression, assignment submissions via official Google Forms, and capstone project portfolios.
  • Authentication Information: Secure cryptographic session tokens generated when you sign in to the Student Portal.
  • AI Assistant Conversations: Questions asked to Penny AI during active sessions, used solely to generate helpful immediate responses.
Section 3

3. How We Use Student Information

Your personal data is used strictly for legitimate educational and administrative purposes:

  • Enrollment & Verification: Reviewing applications, approving admissions, and assigning official Student IDs.
  • Student Portal Access: Authenticating students to watch cohort lecture replays and submit coursework.
  • Academic Mentorship: Reviewing student assignments, evaluating capstone campaigns, and providing feedback.
  • Certification: Verifying completion criteria and issuing the official Pencraft Academy Certificate of Completion.
  • Important Announcements: Sending class schedules, session reminders, and cohort notifications via official WhatsApp or email.
Section 4

4. Student Records & Registry Management

Student admissions and registry records are maintained using secure, restricted Google Cloud spreadsheets accessible only by verified Pencraft Academy faculty and administrators.

When you submit an application through our official Google Form, your details are synchronized to our student registry to enable seamless sign-in on the Student Portal. Our server reads student records over encrypted SSL connections and never exposes raw spreadsheet data to public visitors.

Section 5

5. Student Portal Security & Session Handling

We implement technical safeguards to ensure that student sessions remain secure:

  • HMAC-SHA256 Session Tokens: We issue tamper-proof signed session tokens that expire after 7 days, eliminating the need to transmit unencrypted passwords.
  • Brute Force & Rate Limit Protection: Our backend monitors failed sign-in attempts and enforces progressive rate-limiting lockouts to prevent unauthorized access attempts.
  • Local Storage: Authentication tokens are stored securely in your browser's local storage and can be cleared at any time by clicking Sign Out.
Section 6

6. Third-Party Educational Integrations

To deliver rich interactive learning, Pencraft Academy integrates trusted services:

  • Google Workspace (Drive & Forms): Class video recordings are embedded from secure Google Drive links, and assignment submissions are handled through Google Forms.
  • Google Gemini AI: Powers Penny AI to provide 24/7 educational guidance. Inquiries sent to Penny are processed strictly to generate answers.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): Our primary communication channel for student inquiries, announcements, and direct faculty support.
Section 7

7. Strict Pledge: No Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize student contact information, phone numbers, or email addresses to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties under any circumstances.

Your contact information is used exclusively by Pencraft Academy faculty to communicate with you regarding your education and cohort activities.

Section 8

8. Data Retention & Storage

We retain student records and graduation archives to allow lifelong verification of your earned Certificate of Completion. If an employer or institution requests proof of your credentials in the future, our registry verifies your graduation record.

Applicants who do not complete enrollment or withdraw from courses may request that their personal data be updated or archived.

Section 9

9. Your Privacy Rights

Every Pencraft Academy student has full control over their personal records:

  • Access & Verification: You have the right to request a summary of your recorded details and assignment grades.
  • Correction & Updates: If your phone number, email, or legal name spelling changes, you can request an immediate update via official WhatsApp support.
  • Opt-Out: You may opt out of non-essential academy communications at any time while retaining access to mandatory course requirements.
