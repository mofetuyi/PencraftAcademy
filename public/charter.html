Pencraft Academy Foundational Charter

Prepared by: Osuigwe Wisdom Munachimso (Prince Noble) • 30 June 2026

This document is a foundational proposal to be discussed and adopted by the founding team when Pencraft Academy resumes. It sets forth our institutional rules, safeguards student rights, and establishes an ethical standard for digital learning.

Checks & Balances

Institutional Governance

🏛️

Governance Structure

  • Founder & Director: Strategic vision, partnerships, and institutional development.
  • General Manager: Operations, daily follow-ups, and community coordination.
  • General Secretary: Archives, documentation, meeting minutes, and compliance.
  • Academic Leads: Curriculum oversight, faculty mentorship, and learning quality.
  • Tutors: Classroom instruction, assignment marking, and personal feedback.
  • Student Support Lead: Advocacy, issue resolution, and feedback surveys.
🛡️

Student & Member Rights

  • Right to fair, respectful, and dignified treatment at all times.
  • Clear, transparent expectations regarding assessments and schedules.
  • Prompt, constructive feedback on submitted coursework.
  • Protection of personal data and confidential submissions.
  • The right to a fair hearing before any disciplinary measure is applied.
  • Protection against arbitrary fines, harassment, or retaliation.
⚖️

Disciplinary Process

Discipline is corrective and proportionate, never punitive or arbitrary.

  • Level 1 Verbal Caution: For first-time minor oversights.
  • Level 2 Written Warning: For recurring neglect of expectations.
  • Level 3 Probation & Plan: Structured goals with fixed review date.
  • Level 4 Suspension: Temporary hold to protect community integrity.
  • Level 5 Removal: Reserved for grave misconduct after impartial review.
🤖

Responsible AI Usage Policy

Section 7, Rule 4 of our Charter regulates technology use:

  • Students may use AI when expressly permitted by assignment rubrics.
  • Fact-checking, verification, and critical validation are mandatory.
  • AI contribution must be transparently declared where required.
  • Submitting raw AI output as independent human authorship is prohibited.
Code of Conduct

Core Obligations

👥 Founding Team Rules (Summary)

  • Understand and actively advance the academy's purpose.
  • Fulfill agreed responsibilities and meet agreed deadlines.
  • Attend leadership meetings or communicate absence early.
  • Uphold transparent financial accounting and stewardship.
  • Protect confidential student records and data privacy.
  • Disclose any conflicts of interest candidly.

🎓 Student Code of Conduct (Summary)

  • Participate actively in exercises, discussions, and tasks.
  • Submit authentic, honest work—plagiarism is strictly barred.
  • Treat all tutors, guests, and fellow learners with dignity.
  • Use official academy chat groups strictly for learning.
  • Deliver accurate information during admission and verification.
  • Respect intellectual property and confidential course material.
Section 15 • Official Pledge

The Pencraft Academy Pledge

"I commit to learning, growth, integrity, and service. I will respect knowledge, value discipline, take responsibility for my actions, and treat others with dignity. I will work honestly, use technology responsibly, accept constructive feedback, and strive to improve through practice. I will contribute positively to Pencraft Academy and uphold its commitment to turning ideas into skills and turning skills into value."
