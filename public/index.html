About Pencraft Academy

Our Identity

Pencraft Academy is a learning and skills-development initiative that aims to help learners gain practical knowledge, develop useful skills, and prepare for educational, professional, and entrepreneurial opportunities.

The academy focuses on practical learning, assignments, feedback, collaboration, and responsible use of technology.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible, practical, and ethical skills education that helps learners turn ideas into abilities and use those abilities to create value.

  • Help learners develop useful skills through practice
  • Encourage creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving
  • Promote honesty and responsible use of AI and digital tools
  • Help learners prepare portfolios and pursue legitimate opportunities
  • Build a respectful and supportive learning community
🔭

Our Vision

To become a trusted skills-development academy that helps people grow into capable, creative, disciplined, and ethical individuals who use their skills to create opportunities.

  • Provide useful, practical learning
  • Develop independent thinkers and responsible creators
  • Support learners preparing for work, study, or entrepreneurship
  • Build a dependable team and sustainable programmes
💡

Our Purpose

Pencraft Academy exists to help close the gap between a person's potential and their practical ability by creating opportunities to learn, practise, and improve valuable skills.

  • Help learners identify and develop their abilities
  • Connect knowledge with practical tasks and projects
  • Promote integrity, discipline, and accountability
  • Encourage collaboration and lifelong learning

Our Foundation

Foundational Charter

Our charter establishes the rules, rights, and governance that guide Pencraft Academy.

📜

Governance Structure

  • Founder & Director: Strategic direction and institutional development
  • General Manager: Daily coordination and follow-up
  • General Secretary: Records, minutes, and official documentation
  • Programme Leads: Curriculum, tutor support, and learning quality
  • Tutors: Lessons, assignments, assessments, and feedback
  • Student Support Lead: Student feedback and participation support
🛡️

Your Rights

  • Fair and respectful treatment
  • Clear information about duties and assessments
  • A reasonable chance to respond to allegations
  • Constructive feedback and learning support
  • Protection of personal information
  • Freedom from harassment and retaliation
⚖️

Disciplinary Process

  • L1 Verbal caution for minor first-time issues
  • L2 Written warning for repeated minor issues
  • L3 Improvement plan with clear goals and review date
  • L4 Temporary suspension when needed
  • L5 Removal for serious misconduct after fair review
✊

Our Pledge

"I commit to learning, growth, integrity, and service. I will respect knowledge, value discipline, take responsibility for my actions, and treat others with dignity. I will work honestly, use technology responsibly, accept constructive feedback, and strive to improve through practice."
