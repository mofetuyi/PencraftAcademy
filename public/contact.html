Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a course?

Click any of the gold "Apply for a Course" buttons across the website to open our official Google Form. Fill in your details and submit. Our admissions lead will reach out with confirmation details.

What courses are currently open?

Currently, our active program is Digital Marketing. Upcoming programs in Writing & Editing, Research & Critical Thinking, AI Tools, and Freelancing will launch in upcoming cohorts.

How are certificates awarded?

Certificates reflect actual demonstrated competence across assignments and a capstone portfolio, graded across Master (70–100%), Senior (60–69%), Intermediate (50–59%), and Apprentice (40–49%).
